# TikTok business model, creator money, commerce and music: lessons for TNL Labs (as of Oct 2026)

Notes: ByteDance is private. Every ByteDance or TikTok revenue figure below comes from leaks (Bloomberg, The Information) or third-party estimates. None is audited. "Historical" marks items that are no longer current. The Instagram report (reports/Instagram lessons for TNL Labs.md) already covers Instagram's numbers, Meta-style creator bonuses, Etsy/Depop/BeatStars fee comparisons, sponsored events, tips and affiliate referral share. These notes add only the TikTok-specific evidence.

## 1. Revenue, users and ad products

### Takeaway
ByteDance's revenue went from about $155B (2024) to a reported ~$200B (2025), and a reported ~$120B in H1 2026. That puts it level with Meta (~$201B in 2025). TikTok earns far less per user than Meta, and a growing share of its money comes from commerce and LIVE gifting rather than ads alone.

### Inferences
- **Revenue per user.** Global ad revenue of about $32B over about 1.9B monthly users is roughly $17 per user per year. Meta's revenue per daily active person is $57. TikTok makes up the gap with commerce take rates and roughly 50% of LIVE gifting, not ads alone. (My calculation; the inputs are estimates, and monthly users vs daily people is not like-for-like.)
- **Spark Ads matter most for TNL.** Paying to boost a creator's own post blurs organic and paid. That is exactly what TNL's ad-free "no bought reach" stance rejects.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2023 ByteDance total. A "$110B" figure was unsourced, and the 29% growth rate implies about $120B.
- No TikTok-only (ex-Douyin) gifting revenue for 2025.
- No official monthly or daily user figures. TikTok last said "1B+" monthly users (2021) and about 170M US users (2024 court filings, not re-verified here).

## 2. Creator monetization

### Takeaway
TikTok has replaced its main creator payout programme three times since 2020:
1. Creator Fund (2020): a fixed pool, pennies per 1,000 views.
2. Creativity Program Beta (2023).
3. Creator Rewards Program (2024): videos over 1 minute, about $0.40–$1.00 RPM (estimates).

LIVE gifting (TikTok keeps about 50% nominally, closer to 75–80% of what the viewer paid once store fees and coin markup are counted) and Shop affiliate commissions are where real creator money is. Creator trust is low because of opaque formulas and shifting rules.

### Cited Findings
- **Creator Fund (historical, Jul 2020 – Dec 16 2023 in the US):** a $200M US pool, growing to $1B over three years and later $2B globally (TikTok's figures). Creators reported payouts "in the pennies" per 1,000 views, and Hank Green said he earned 2.5¢ per 1,000. Because the pool was fixed, each creator's share shrank as more creators joined. Tubefilter estimated about $550K/day split across all US creators. TikTok never disclosed the formula. — [Tubefilter 2023](https://tubefilter.com/2023/11/07/tiktok-shutting-down-creator-fund-creativity-program/); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90979212/tiktok-billion-dollar-creator-fund-ending); [Tubefilter 2020](https://tubefilter.com/2020/09/11/tiktok-denies-creator-fund-diminishes-views/)
- **Creativity Program Beta (2023) → Creator Rewards Program (renamed around Mar 2024):**
  - Only videos over 1 minute qualify.
  - The formula weighs originality, play duration, search value and engagement.
  - A qualified view lasts at least 5s, comes from the For You feed, and isn't boosted by paid promotion.
  - Eligibility: 18+, 10K followers and 100K views in the last 30 days.
  - TikTok claimed "up to 20× more" than the Creator Fund.
  - Creators who enrol can't go back to the Creator Fund.
  — [ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/tiktok-creativity-program); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90979212/tiktok-billion-dollar-creator-fund-ending); [TechCrunch via mirror](https://blog.kowatek.com/?p=16416)
- **Creator Rewards RPM (estimates):** commonly $0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views. Some put it at $0.30–$0.60. Old Creator Fund payouts were $0.02–$0.04. Since 2025, "Additional Rewards" sit on top of the base payout (a single marketing-blog claim). — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/); [Elev8or](https://www.elev8or.io/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-explained)
- **LIVE gifts:** viewers buy Coins, send Gifts, and the creator receives Diamonds, which they cash out.
  - Widely reported split: about 2 coins = 1 diamond, so TikTok keeps about 50%. Not confirmed by TikTok.
  - One analysis: measured against what the viewer actually paid, the creator gets about 24% for coins bought on the web and under 20% for coins bought in the app.
  - Withdrawal minimums vary by source ($50–$100).
  — [streamer.guide](https://streamer.guide/guides/tiktok-live-gifts-coins-diamonds); [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/tiktok-live-gifts/); [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/calculators/diamond-converter/)
- **Series (paywalled collections, Mar 2023):**
  - Up to 80 videos of up to 20 minutes each, priced $0.99–$189.99.
  - Select creators only.
  - Creators keep 100% "for a limited time", minus app store and processing fees (likely more than 30% in the app).
  - TikTok never said what its later cut would be.
  — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/tiktoks-series-feature-will-allow-creators-to-charge-for-premium-content-140033561.html); [Tech.co](https://tech.co/news/tiktoks-new-series-feature); [The Leap](https://www.theleap.co/tiktok-series-paywalled-content/)
- **Tips:** TechCrunch reported a test of profile Tips (outside LIVE) for creators with 100K+ followers. I found no reliable source on whether tips were ended. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2260193)
- **Shop affiliate:** creators earned roughly 5–20% commission per product at the 2024 fee change. Vendor blogs cite an average US rate of about 13% in 2026 (no primary source). — [Passionfruit](https://passionfru.it/tiktok-shop-bigger-cut-from-sellers-34244/); [Darkroom](https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/tiktok-shop-fees-seller-cost-breakdown-2026)

### Inferences
- **The fixed pool was the original sin.** With a fixed pool, more creators means less money each, so creators were effectively competing against each other. Rewards later moved to a revenue-like RPM, but the formula stays a black box. A clear % commission like TNL's is the opposite design and should be marketed as such.
- **TikTok now pushes creators toward commerce.** Its money flows reward selling products (affiliate) and LIVE performance (gifts), not making art. For a creatives-first community, that is a warning sign.

### Gaps
- No verified data from TikTok One / Creator Marketplace (rates, take).
- No verified status for LIVE Subscriptions (launched 2022), Series' current split, or Tips in 2026.
- No reliable creator-sentiment survey from 2025–26 found in this pass.

## 3. TikTok Shop

### Takeaway
- **Launch:** US, Sept 2023 (historical).
- **GMV:** US about $9B (2024) → $15.1B (2025, +68%). Global about $64.3B (2025, +94%). All are Momentum Works estimates.
- **Fees:** the US referral fee rose from 2% + $0.30 to 6% (Apr 2024) and then 8% (Jul 2024), as subsidies were cut.
- **Why it worked where Instagram's Shop tab failed:** creator affiliate commissions plus subsidies plus in-feed checkout plus live selling.

### Cited Findings
- **GMV (Momentum Works/Tabcut estimates):**
  - Global $64.3B in 2025 (+94%, 16 markets).
  - US $15.1B, up from $9B in 2024. 2024 fell short of an internal target of $17.5B (per Sacra).
  - Southeast Asia $45.6B, of which Indonesia $13.1B.
  - US H1 2025: $5.8B.
  — [DealStreetAsia](https://cdn.dealstreetasia.com/stories/tiktok-shop-gmv-2025-472662); [Momentum Works](https://thelowdown.momentum.asia/new-report-tiktok-shop-u-s-gmv-grew-68-to-reach-us15-1b-in-2025/); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/bytedance)
  - A competing claim (Jiemian, unnamed sources) says global GMV neared $100B in 2025. Unverified. — [Jiemian](https://en.jiemian.com/article/13852848.html)
- **Where US sales happen (2025):** video 50% (down from 58%), the Shop tab 36% (up from 32%), LIVE 14% (up from 10%). — [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/tiktok-shop-data-analysis/)
- **Sales are concentrated:** more than half of stores made no sales. 2,143 stores passed $1M and 135 passed $10M. November–December was about 30% of annual GMV. — [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/tiktok-shop-data-analysis/)
- **Fees and subsidies:**
  - The 2% + $0.30 fee went to 6% on Apr 1 2024 and 8% on Jul 1 2024.
  - Electronics and collectibles stay at 6%; pre-owned at 5%.
  - Reported by The Information (Jan 2024) alongside cuts to merchant subsidies.
  — [Tech.co](https://tech.co/news/tiktok-shop-hiking-us-seller-fees); [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/sydney-sladovnik/tiktok-shop-is-raising-referral-fees.html); [Passionfruit](https://passionfru.it/tiktok-shop-bigger-cut-from-sellers-34244/)
  - Conflicting: one agency says the US standard fee went from 6% to 8% on Aug 4 2026, while TikTok's public table (May 2026) still shows 6% for most categories. Unverified. — [beBOLD](https://www.bebolddigital.com/news/tiktok-shop-8-percent-referral-fee)
- **Instagram contrast:** Instagram removed its Shop tab in 2023 (see the Instagram report).

### Inferences
- **What worked:**
  - Commission for third parties (affiliate creators).
  - Subsidies at launch, then gradual fee increases once sellers were locked in.
  - Checkout inside the content.
  Instagram had none of the affiliate economics.
- **Two lessons for TNL:**
  - Its sliding 10% → 2% is the reverse of TikTok's "subsidize then raise" pattern. That is a trust advantage worth stating publicly.
  - An affiliate share for members who tag other members' listings borrows the part of TikTok Shop that worked.
- **Expect a long tail with nothing in it.** More than half of TikTok stores sold nothing. TNL should expect most listings to sit idle and design discovery (events, labs) to send buyers to them.

### Gaps
- TikTok Shop's own revenue and losses: The Information reported losses, but the article is paywalled and wasn't retrieved.
- US 2026 GMV beyond unsourced vendor claims (e.g. "$32B H1 2026").

## 4. Music

### Takeaway
TikTok is the biggest music-discovery engine but pays labels flat-fee-style licences.
- **UMG blackout:** Universal pulled its catalogue from Feb 1 2024 and returned in early May 2024 with a deal for "improved remuneration" and AI protections (terms undisclosed).
- **TikTok Music:** shut down Nov 28 2024.
- **SoundOn (distribution):** continues.
- **Discovery data:** TikTok-commissioned Luminate data shows users are about 2× likelier to discover and share music.

### Cited Findings
- **UMG dispute (historical):**
  - The licence expired Jan 31 2024. UMG's open letter cited pay, AI, and user safety.
  - A new deal was announced May 1–2 2024. It covers recordings and publishing, with "improved remuneration" tied to e-commerce, and adds Add to Music App, analytics, ticketing and generative-AI protections.
  - Muted videos were unmuted within 1–2 weeks. No rates were disclosed.
  — [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/universal-music-and-tiktok-strike-new-licensing-deal/); [Telecoms.com](https://telecoms.com/telecoms-law/universal-music-group-and-tiktok-bury-the-hatchet-with-new-licensing-deal)
- **TikTok Music (ex-Resso):** shutdown announced Sep 24 2024, closed Nov 28 2024, "to focus on driving listening on streaming services". SoundOn distribution continues. — [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2024/09/24/tiktok-music-shutdown/); [Techloy](https://www.techloy.com/tiktoks-music-platform-is-shutting-down/amp/)
- **SoundOn terms (at its 2022 launch, may be dated):** artists kept 100% of royalties in year one, then 90%. — [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2024/09/24/tiktok-music-shutdown/)
- **Luminate Music Impact Report (TikTok-commissioned; Jul 2022 – Jun 2023):**
  - US TikTok users are nearly 2× likelier than average social / short-video users to discover music, and to both discover and share it.
  - UK TikTok users spend 49% more on music.
  - Engagement on TikTok "strongly correlated" with streaming volumes.
  — [Report PDF via MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/files/2023/11/TikTok-Luminate-Report-2023-1.pdf); [Music Week](https://www.musicweek.com/digital/read/tiktok-publishes-impact-report-on-music-discovery-and-consumption/088895)
  - A claim that 84% of 2024 Billboard Global 200 entries went viral on TikTok first is unverified. — [Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/how-tiktok-is-changing-the-music-industry)

### Inferences
- **Artists don't get paid for virality.** Value goes to discovery and is collected elsewhere, on streaming services. Labels fight over the size of the licence, not per-use pay to artists.
- **TNL's music lab can do the opposite:** turn discovery into a direct sale or tip to the beat-maker at the point of discovery (Stripe Connect), and show which events drove plays.
- **Learn from TikTok Music's shutdown.** A platform that drives discovery struggled to keep listeners. TNL shouldn't try to be a streaming service; it should link out and sell directly.

### Gaps
- Commercial Music Library (pre-cleared music for business accounts): no current-source detail retrieved.
- Billboard/Luminate 2025–26 TikTok-to-chart data.
- What artists actually earn per TikTok use.

## 5. Regulatory and business risk

### Takeaway
The US divest-or-ban law (2024) ended with TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC, which closed Jan 22 2026:
- Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX hold 15% each.
- ByteDance keeps 19.9%.
- A majority-American board of seven oversees US data, the app and the algorithm.

In the EU, TikTok Lite's watch-to-earn Rewards were permanently withdrawn under the Digital Services Act in Aug 2024.

### Cited Findings
- **JV closed:** Jan 22 2026, announced Jan 23 2026.
  - Investors: 80.1% American and global, ByteDance 19.9%.
  - The other investors are Dell, Vastmere, Alpha Wave, Revolution, Merritt Way, Via Nova, Virgo LI and NJJ.
  - Both the US and Chinese governments signed off.
  - It followed Trump's September 2025 executive order.
  — [Axios](https://axios.com/2026/01/23/tiktok-deal-trump-app-ban); [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/tiktok-us-spin-off-finalized-led-by-oracle-silver-lake-and-mgx/); [Variety](https://au.variety.com/2025/digital/global/tiktok-us-joint-venture-deal-close-date-oracle-silver-lake-31293/)
  - Pre-close reports differed on the split between the consortium (50%) and affiliates of existing investors (30.1%).
- **TikTok Lite Rewards (EU, historical):**
  - Adults earned points for watching, liking and following, redeemable for vouchers or TikTok coins, capped at about €1/day and up to an hour of watching.
  - The Commission opened proceedings on Apr 22 2024.
  - TikTok committed on Aug 5 2024 to withdraw the programme permanently and not launch a workaround. This was the first DSA case closed (105 days).
  — [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/tiktok-commits-permanently-withdraw-tiktok-lite-rewards-programme-eu-comply-digital-services-act); [Silicon UK](https://silicon.co.uk/e-marketing/socialmedia/tiktok-rewards-withdraw-dsa-574418); [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2024/08/05/tiktok-commits-to-withdraw-tiktok-lite-from-eu)
- **Minors and LIVE gifting (Utah lawsuit, Jun 2024; unsealed Jan 2025; allegations, not findings):**
  - TikTok's internal "Project Meramec" reportedly found hundreds of thousands of 13–15-year-olds hosting LIVE despite the 18+ rule.
  - Adults reportedly paid them gifts to perform sexualized acts, with TikTok keeping up to half.
  - TikTok says the complaint cherry-picks outdated documents.
  — [Utah AG](https://attorneygeneral.utah.gov/utah-dcp-and-ags-office-announce-release-of-previously-redacted-information-tiktok-execs-knew-they-were-profiting-off-the-sexual-exploitation-of-minors); [Utah DCP PDF](https://blog.commerce.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/TikTok-Live-Unsealed-Redaction-Highlights.pdf); [Washington State Standard](https://washingtonstatestandard.com/?p=7687)

### Inferences
- **Both regulator actions targeted the same thing:** paying users to engage (Lite Rewards) and paid gifting from strangers to minors (LIVE). Any TNL tip or gift feature must be adults-only for receiving, verified through Stripe Connect KYC, with no "points for engagement".
- **Paid-entry contests:** see the lottery caution already in the Instagram report.

### Gaps
- How the JV changed US revenue sharing with ByteDance, and the algorithm licence fee: not retrieved.
- The outcome of the Utah case: not checked.
- New EU DSA fines on TikTok in 2025–26: not checked.

## 6. Lessons for TNL (synthesis from the findings above; not repeating the Instagram report)

### Takeaway
Copy TikTok's affiliate economics and discovery-to-purchase loop. Reject its opaque pools, gamified coins, and "subsidize then raise fees" pattern.

### Cited Findings
(These lessons rest on the findings cited in sections 1–5.)
- Creator Fund fixed-pool backlash and secret formula. — [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2023/11/07/tiktok-shutting-down-creator-fund-creativity-program/)
- Coins hide the real take (the creator gets about 20–24% of viewer spend). — [streamer.guide](https://streamer.guide/guides/tiktok-live-gifts-coins-diamonds)
- Fee hike 2% → 8% after subsidies. — [Tech.co](https://tech.co/news/tiktok-shop-hiking-us-seller-fees)
- Affiliate commissions of 5–20% drove Shop. — [Passionfruit](https://passionfru.it/tiktok-shop-bigger-cut-from-sellers-34244/)
- Series kept 100% only "for a limited time". — [Tech.co](https://tech.co/news/tiktoks-new-series-feature)

### Inferences
1. **Listening sessions with tips.** A beat/track premiere room with direct dollar tips, not coins. Show "artist receives $X of your $Y" before payment. Use TNL's normal commission and Stripe fees, so the creator gets about 90–96% vs about 20–24% on TikTok. Receiving tips is 18+ only, and the payee is Stripe-verified.
2. **Paid collections ("Series"-style).** Sample packs, tutorial series, stem bundles sold as one market listing. Lock the take rate in writing; don't promise "100% for a limited time".
3. **Affiliate tagging of other members' listings.** Pay a small share, taken from TNL's fee rather than the seller's, so the seller's take never goes up. Award rep only when the other member accepts the tag, consistent with the collab rule.
4. **Sponsor-funded event prize pools.** Make the prize pool and its source public on the event page. Never use "boost your entry". Votes stay in the app.
5. **Fixed, published take rates with a no-increase promise for existing levels.** This turns TikTok's fee hikes and shifting programmes into TNL's marketing line.
6. **Avoid:**
   - Fixed bonus pools split across creators.
   - Virtual currencies.
   - Gift leaderboards that rank fans by spend.
   - Watch-to-earn.
   - Paying for views (the Creator Rewards model rewards length and retention, not craft).

### Gaps
- Legal specifics need counsel:
  - State sweepstakes/contest law for sponsor prize events (free entry route).
  - Money-transmission questions for tips (Stripe Connect largely covers this).
  - Age rules for payees (Stripe requires account holders to be 18+, or have guardian consent in some regions; not verified here).
