# Instagram Product Features 2019 – October 2026 (emphasis 2023–2026), with TNL verdicts

Research date: 2026-10-07. Sources are a mix of primary (about.fb.com, about.instagram.com) and secondary trade coverage/aggregators (Social Media Today, TechCrunch, Engadget, Metricool, NapoleonCat, SocialPilot, Inro). Aggregator-only claims are flagged "(aggregator)". Items before 2023 are marked HISTORICAL. TNL context was taken from /home/user/TNL-labs/README.md (read-only): TNL has 10-image carousels, collab invites (up to 5), reshares, saves to boards, DMs v2 (groups, reactions, voice notes, forward, mute), Showroom ranked feed, genre labs with channels and pins, music player, market, events/tournaments with voting, drafts + background post queue, photo editor, people tags, places, `/api/search`, admin broadcast. ## Feed and discovery (Explore, search/SEO, Friends tab, Your Algorithm, Following/Favorites, Map, Trial Reels, Reposts)

### Takeaway
2025–2026 Instagram moved discovery in two directions at once: more "friend-graph" surfaces (Reposts tab, Friends tab in Reels, Map, Blend — all launched 6 Aug 2025) and more explicit user control of the recommender ("Your Algorithm", Reels Dec 2025 → Explore → main feed in 2026). For creators it added low-risk testing (Trial Reels, Dec 2024; schedulable Feb 2026) and outside discovery (Google indexing of public professional posts, July 2025). Hashtags were deliberately de-emphasised (hashtag following removed Dec 2024; cap of 5 then 3 hashtags late 2025).

### Cited Findings
**Reposts (launched 6 Aug 2025)**
- Users can repost public Reels and feed posts; reposts appear in a separate "Reposts" profile tab and are recommended to the reposter's followers' feeds; the original creator is credited and can reach the reposter's followers. A "thought bubble" note can be added to a repost. — [About Meta, Aug 2025](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/08/new-instagram-features-help-you-connect/)
- 2026: Instagram's "original content" guidance says accounts that primarily repost photos/carousels are ineligible for recommendations; low-effort edits don't count as original. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- Mosseri said reposting feed posts to Stories does not boost reach. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)

**Friends tab in Reels (6 Aug 2025, global)**
- Shows "public content your friends have interacted with" (liked, commented, reposted, created) plus recommendations from Blends; toggle "Friends" vs "Reels" at top. Controls let users hide their own likes/comments from it and mute activity bubbles from specific followers. — [About Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/08/new-instagram-features-help-you-connect/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-reposts-friends-feed-reels-friend-map/756996/)

**Instagram Map (6 Aug 2025, US first)**
- Location sharing is OFF unless the user opts in; audience = friends (mutual follows), Close Friends, selected people, or no one; can exclude places/people; location updates when the app is opened. Anyone can browse location-tagged Reels/posts/Stories/Notes from accounts they follow on the map, shown 24 hours after posting. Entry point: top of the DM inbox. Teen supervision notifies parents. — [About Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/08/new-instagram-features-help-you-connect/)
- Later update added clearer active/inactive location-sharing indicators after privacy backlash. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)

**Your Algorithm (topic controls)**
- Launched for Reels in Dec 2025, then expanded to Explore and the main feed in 2026; users see topics the system thinks they like and mark "more"/"less". — [Search summary citing TechCrunch / The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/instagram-your-algorithm-central-experience-mosseri)
- June 2026: Mosseri previewed making it "central to your experience" — prototypes include a pull-down gesture in the main feed, swipe-up prompt on individual Reels, and buttons beneath each Reel. — [The Next Web, 28 Jun 2026](https://thenextweb.com/news/instagram-your-algorithm-central-experience-mosseri)
- Rolled out to all English-speaking users, including setting "top 3 interests for 2026"; testing up to 5 interest topics on profiles. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- Entry point: Reels tab, "two hearts" icon. (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/)

**Trial Reels (Dec 2024; scheduling added Feb 2026)**
- A Trial Reel is shown first to non-followers via Reels feed and Explore; it stays off the grid, Reels tab and followers' feeds while on trial. Performance shows in ~24h; creator can share it to followers manually, or set auto-share if it hits chosen metrics within the first 72 hours. Eligibility: professional accounts with ≥200 followers (personal ≥1,000 reported). A flop is not folded into the account's regular ranking signals. — [Outfy / Postfast summary](https://postfa.st/blog/instagram-trial-reels); [Social Media Today launch](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-launches-trial-reels-for-audience-sampling/735136/)
- Feb 2026: Trial Reels can be scheduled; Mosseri said scheduling doesn't hurt reach; a posting cap within a 30-day window was later added. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/); [Disrupt Marketing](https://disruptmarketing.co/blog/instagram-updates/)

**Search / SEO**
- From 10 July 2025, public professional-account posts (Reels, carousels, videos, images) are eligible for Google search indexing; later appearing in Google Discover; testing a knowledge-panel redesign. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/); [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/)
- Hashtag following removed 13 Dec 2024 (existing followed-hashtag posts stop appearing in feed; hashtag search remains). Hashtag cap cut to 5, then to 3 per post/Reel (late Oct 2025). (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)

**Other feed/surface changes**
- Rectangular (4:5-ish vertical) profile grid replaced squares (~Jan 2025) with "Adjust preview" thumbnail control; grid drag-and-drop reorder launched globally in 2026; users can add posts they're tagged in to their own grid (Sept 2026). (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/)
- Watch History (Settings → Your Activity) lists viewed Reels with sort/filter; Reels auto-scroll option; single tap pauses Reels. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- Music-enabled carousels now show in the Reels tab. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- Tests of opening the app to Reels with a "Your Feeds" hub; TV apps (Fire TV Dec 2025, Google TV 2026). — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/); [Disrupt Marketing](https://disruptmarketing.co/blog/instagram-updates/)

### Inferences
- The friend-graph surfaces (Reposts, Friends tab) reuse existing actions (likes, comments, reposts) as a new feed — cheap to build when the activity log already exists. TNL already has likes, comments, reshares and collabs, so a "Friends" view is mostly a query.
- "Your Algorithm" is essentially a visible, editable interest vector. For TNL, labs already are explicit topics; a "more/less of this lab" control on Showroom is a cheap equivalent.
- Trial Reels is the clearest creator-loved discovery feature: it removes the fear that a bad post damages the account. TNL's Showroom ranking could offer a "test with non-followers" mode with little UI.

**TNL verdicts**
- Reposts tab — ADAPT: TNL has reshares; add a profile "Reposts" tab and an optional note. Keep original-credit; rep must still go to the original author only.
- Friends tab — ADOPT (small): "what people you follow liked/commented/collabed on", with an opt-out for hiding your own activity.
- Your Algorithm — ADAPT: per-lab "more/less" in Showroom; labs are already topics.
- Trial Reels / "test post" — ADAPT later: needs reach measurement first (see insights).
- Map — SKIP: privacy and moderation risk outweigh value for a small creative network; TNL already has places.
- Google indexing / SEO — ADOPT: TNL already has public `/u/:name` and `/p/:id` pages; ensure titles/OG/alt text/sitemap.
- Hashtag following — SKIP (Instagram removed it; labs/channels already cover topic following).
- Grid reorder + tagged-in posts on grid — ADOPT (cheap, creatives curate portfolios).

### Gaps
- No official Meta numbers on Your Algorithm usage, Map adoption, or Repost volume were found.
- "Favorites"/"Following" chronological feeds (launched March 2022, HISTORICAL) — I did not re-verify their 2026 status in this pass; iPad app reportedly has Following-tab filtering (Metricool).
- Explore redesign details 2023–2026 not researched.

## Creation (carousels, Edits, templates, Add Yours, collabs, Remix, drafts/scheduling, alt text, captions, music, Notes, Stories)

### Takeaway
Instagram's creation surface grew longer, more editable and more AI-assisted: carousels 10→20 (Aug 2024), Reels up to 3 min then 20-min recording, the standalone Edits app (Apr 2025) and desktop Edits (Jun 2026), post-publish editing (swap audio, Jul 2026; start a collab on an already-published post), per-slide carousel captions, and comment images/editing. Stories gained social stickers and paid perks via Instagram Plus.

### Cited Findings
- **Carousels**: expanded from 10 to 20 slides (Aug 2024). Per-slide captions (dropdown: one caption or one per slide) in 2026. (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **Replace audio on published feed posts/carousels** (21 Jul 2026) without losing likes, comments, shares or reach. (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/)
- **Edits app** launched mid-April 2025 (free; cutout AI, green screen, timeline, teleprompter, Inspiration tab, insights). Aug 2026 update: compare up to 3 Reels side-by-side, audience insights, bilingual captions in 15 languages, "Restyle" from text prompts, project versions, template overlays, locked clips. June 2026: Edits desktop synced with mobile + AI assistant suggesting content from views/retention/trending audio. Aug 2026 "First Draft": AI assembles a rough cut from selected clips (iPhone first). Shareable PDF insights for media kits. (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **Reels length/camera**: Reels expanded to 3 minutes (from 90s), then up to 20-minute recording; undo, touch-up slider, native teleprompter. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **Series** (2 Jun 2026, test): bundle episodic Reels with their own page/profile tab; limited creators. Separately, "Reel series linking" adds a next-episode button. (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **Collab posts**: up to 5 collaborators on a post or Reel; invitee gets a notification and the post appears on their grid only once they accept; invites are sent at publish time, not when scheduled; private accounts can only invite accounts that follow them, and a public account accepting makes it publicly visible on that account. — [Sked Social / Metricool collab guides](https://metricool.com/instagram-collaborative-posts)
- Collabs can now be initiated on already-published posts (late 2025). (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **Scheduling**: native scheduling up to 75 days ahead (collab invites still require being present at publish). — [Metricool collab guide](https://metricool.com/instagram-collaborative-posts); scheduling and Insights opened to all public accounts (not just professional) in 2026 (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **Add Yours sticker** gained video remix: insert your clip matching the original sequence with audio sync (late 2025). Animated Story collages auto-combine 5–20 photos. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **Notes on posts/Reels** (Aug 2024): up to 60 characters, visible 3 days, to mutual followers or Close Friends. (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/)
- **Story comments** (Sept 2024): public comments on Stories visible for 24h. (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/)
- **Instants** (2026): unedited photos to Close Friends or mutuals, disappear after opening / max 24h, screenshot-proof, with a standalone app. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/); [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/)
- **Comments**: photo comments; 15-minute text-only comment editing window; pinned comments; dislike button for comments tested. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **Profile cards** (Nov 2024): two-sided digital business card with bio, links, music, QR code. (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/)
- **Swap** (let users replace text overlay on others' Reels) briefly appeared then was quietly removed from the Help Center. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **Auto-translation** of Reel captions/stickers (15+ languages) and AI voice translation with lip-sync (English/Spanish, ≥1,000 followers, not in EU/UK/Brazil). (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)

### Inferences
- The strongest creator-side pattern is "edit after publish without losing engagement" (audio swap, add collab later, thumbnail edit, reorder grid). TNL can do this cheaply because it owns the post row.
- Instagram keeps adding AI generation; for a "by creatives" network, AI generation conflicts with the positioning (Mosseri himself pivoted to "raw, real human content" for 2026 per [Disrupt Marketing](https://disruptmarketing.co/blog/instagram-updates/)).

**TNL verdicts**
- Carousels to 20 — ADAPT: raise 10→20 only if upload/storage cost is fine; per-slide captions are a nicer win for portfolios.
- Collabs (5, accept-to-appear, add to existing post) — ADAPT: TNL already matches 5 + accept; add "invite a collaborator to an already-published post".
- Scheduling (75 days) — ADOPT: TNL has drafts and a post queue; add a publish-at time (server tick, like `tickEvent()`).
- Replace audio after publish / edit after publish — ADOPT: TNL has a sound library; editing the sound on a live post is low effort.
- Edits-style video editor / AI First Draft / Restyle — SKIP: heavy, and AI-generation is off-brand; keep the photo editor.
- Add Yours / prompt chains — ADAPT: fits labs (e.g. "show your desk"), could tie into events.
- Notes (60 chars, 3 days) — ADAPT: a short status on profile/inbox suits musicians ("in the studio") — low cost.
- Instants / disappearing photos — SKIP: off-mission.
- Series (episodic) — ADAPT later: a profile "series" grouping is basically a board; TNL boards may cover it.
- Comment photos, 15-min comment edit, pinned comments — ADOPT (small, high utility).
- Alt text — ADOPT for accessibility + SEO (see gaps).
- Remix / Swap — SKIP (Swap killed; remix rights messy for creatives' work).

## Community and messaging (broadcast channels, Close Friends, DMs, comments, Blend)

### Takeaway
Instagram's centre of gravity moved to DMs: Mosseri has repeatedly said sharing moved from feed to Stories and especially to DMs, and DM "sends" became a top ranking signal. DMs gained scheduling, translation, pinning (3 per chat), drawing/stickers, nicknames, folders/filters and Blend; broadcast channels (2023) gave creators a one-to-many inbox channel.

### Cited Findings
- **Broadcast channels**: tested Feb 2023, expanded globally 15 Jun 2023. Creator posts text, photos, video, voice notes and polls into followers' inbox; followers can react and vote but not reply (one-way). Channels can be open (anyone following can join) or subscriber-only. Joined via Story sticker, profile link, or a one-time notification. — [Instagram announcement](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/introducing-broadcast-channels/); [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-broadcast-channels/)
- Claim that broadcast channel users see "3x higher engagement" is from a third-party blog attributing it to Meta; not verified at source. — [ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/instagram-broadcast-channels) (treat as unverified)
- **DM features** (2025): schedule a message (long-press send), pin up to 3 items per chat, translate a message, share songs (30-sec preview), draw on the DM screen and place stickers, nicknames per chat (Oct 2024), filters (Story replies, unread, unanswered, followers, verified) and custom folders. (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **E2EE DMs discontinued** from 8 May 2026. (aggregator, single source) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **Blend** (2025): a shared Reels feed inside a 1:1 or group DM via invite; "mixed reception". (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **Close Friends**: testing letting users remove themselves from someone's Close Friends list; Instagram Plus adds multiple story audience lists. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/); [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2187830/the-instagram-plus-subscription-has-officially-launched)
- **Mosseri**: sharing among friends "is moving from feed to stories, and even more from feed to DMs". Third-party figure of 3.8bn Reels reshared via DM daily is unverified at source. — [Search summary (eMarketer / TechCrunch)](https://www.emarketer.com/content/instagram-experiments-with-reels-default-home-screen)
- Live requires ≥1,000 followers (2025). (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)

### Inferences
- TNL already has a strong DM base (groups, reactions, voice notes, forward, mute), plus lab channels with pins, so it is ahead of Instagram's 2023 baseline. Missing cheap wins: scheduled send, pin-in-DM, inbox filters.
- Broadcast channels map almost exactly onto TNL lab channels plus admin broadcast; a per-creator one-way channel would let artists announce drops to fans.

**TNL verdicts**
- Broadcast channels — ADAPT: a creator "announcements" channel on the profile (one-way, reactions + polls), reusing lab-channel code.
- Close Friends / audience lists — SKIP for now (no Stories on TNL).
- DM schedule / pin (3) / inbox filters — ADOPT (small).
- DM translation — SKIP (needs external service; rule: no new services without asking).
- Blend — SKIP (mixed reception, and TNL has no Reels firehose).
- Hidden words / restrict / comment limits — ADOPT hidden words + restrict as a safety basic (see gaps for mechanics).

- No primary numbers on broadcast channel adoption.

## Creator tools (Insights metrics, Professional dashboard, partnerships, creator marketplace, subscriptions, gifts)

### Takeaway
On 21 Apr 2025 Meta made **Views** the primary metric across all formats (replacing Impressions and Reel Plays in Insights); Reels insights then added **skip rate** (share of viewers who scroll away in the first 3 seconds), retention, sends-per-reach and follower-driver breakdowns. Monetisation shifted from shopping to creator payments: subscriptions, gifts/Stars ($0.01 per star), photo/carousel bonuses (testing), plus a paid consumer tier (Instagram Plus, $3.99/mo, launched 2026).

### Cited Findings
**Metric definitions**
- Views (from 21 Apr 2025) = number of times content was on screen or (Reels) started to play or replay; repeat views count; replaces Impressions and Plays in Insights; Impressions remain in Ads Manager. Mosseri: "We want to evolve this so it's easier to understand how your content is doing regardless of the format." — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/instagram-marketing/instagram-views-metrics-changes); [IZEA](https://izea.com/resources/instagram-views-metrics-changes/)
- Accounts reached = unique accounts that had the content on screen, whether or not they played it. — [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-insights)
- Watch time = total time watched; average watch time = watch time ÷ first-time plays; retention curve = % still watching at each second. — [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-insights)
- Skip rate = % of viewers who scrolled past within the first 3 seconds; surfaced first in "What impacts your views". Third-party claim that 30–40% is "healthy" is not an Instagram figure. — [Inro / search summary](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-insights)
- Sends = times the post was sent via DM; profile visits, follows and link taps attributable to a post; view sources split into followers' feed, Reels tab, Explore, search, profile, shares into Stories. — [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-insights)
- 2026 insights redesign: tabs for engagement and audience demographics; share rate, skip rate, views over time; Reel/carousel-specific likes, post demographics, "top follower drivers", "viewers". Competitive insights: compare up to 10 accounts in the Professional Dashboard. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- Mosseri reiterated (March 2026) watch time and sends are the dominant Reels ranking signals; Dec 2025 update weighted DM sends more heavily. (aggregator) — [search summary of dataslayer/Metricool](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)

**Monetisation**
- Gifts: viewers buy Stars; creator earns $0.01 per Star; payout once balance ≥$25; requires professional account, 18+, ≥5,000 followers, selected regions. — [search summary of creator monetisation guides / SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/instagram-launches-new-ways-for-creators-to-earn-revenue/470028/)
- Subscriptions: monthly fee for exclusive content (subscriber-only Stories, Lives, broadcast channels). — [same](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/instagram-launches-new-ways-for-creators-to-earn-revenue/470028/); [Instagram broadcast channels post](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/introducing-broadcast-channels/)
- Photo & carousel bonuses (testing): regional view thresholds (e.g., US 5M), up to 150 recent posts per two-week period, 28-day earning window, excludes branded/boosted/collab posts. (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/)
- Affiliate links in Reels; Meta Verified link tiers ($14.99 no links → $349.99 six links per Reel) and Meta One tiers ($14.99–$499/mo, links capped at 12 posts + 12 Reels/month). (aggregator) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- Creator Marketplace revamp: intent-based recommendations, "Similar Creators" search, ad performance badges. (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- **Instagram Plus** (2026, $3.99/mo; ₹99 in India): Story Spotlight, Story Extend (48h), multiple audience lists, story preview, rewatch stats, search your viewer list, anonymous story viewing, custom app icons and bio font, 6 profile pins, super hearts. — [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2187830/the-instagram-plus-subscription-has-officially-launched); [T2 Online](https://t2online.in/tech/tech-news/instagram-plus-rolls-out-globally-with-new-premium-features/2005475)

### Inferences
- The minimum viable insights set for TNL is: views (on-screen count), reach (unique viewers), interactions (likes, comments, saves to boards, sends via DM, reshares), profile visits and follows from post, plus view source (Showroom / lab / profile / DM / external page). All except "on screen" are already events TNL records; views need a lightweight impression log (one row per user-post-day is enough).
- Skip rate / watch time only matter for video; TNL's music player could report "plays" and "% listened" instead — more relevant to musicians than skip rate.
- Gifts/subscriptions touch real money; TNL already has Stripe Connect, so a "tip" or "support" is feasible but is a Careful Area.

**TNL verdicts**
- Post insights (views, reach, interactions, sends, saves, profile visits, follows-from-post, sources) — ADOPT, highest priority (TNL's main gap).
- Account dashboard (followers over time, top posts) — ADOPT after post insights.
- Skip rate / retention — ADAPT: track-level "plays / completion" for the music player instead.
- Competitive insights — SKIP (small network; creates comparison anxiety).
- Gifts / Stars — ADAPT carefully: simple tips via Stripe Connect; avoid virtual currency.
- Subscriptions — SKIP for now (moderation, payouts, and small audience sizes).
- Branded content / creator marketplace — SKIP now; revisit when brands arrive (TNL Market covers commerce).
- Bonuses — SKIP (needs ad revenue).
- Paid consumer tier (Instagram Plus) — SKIP: anonymous story viewing etc. are trust-negative; TNL has no Stories.

- No primary help.instagram.com fetch was done this pass for exact metric wording; definitions come from reputable secondary sources and match Meta's April 2025 change.
- Subscription creator earnings / adoption numbers not found.
- Exact branded-content tool mechanics (Paid partnership label, partnership ads) not re-verified.

## Biggest measurable impact vs flops

### Takeaway
The measurable winners were Reels (≈50% of time spent, per Mosseri-attributed figures) and DM sharing (sends now a top ranking signal). The clearest flops were separate destinations that pulled users out of the main habit loop: IGTV (killed 2022), the Shop tab and Live Shopping (removed Feb/Mar 2023), Guides, Blend (mixed), Swap (quietly removed), and the 2022 full-screen feed test.

### Cited Findings
- Reels account for ~50% of time spent on Instagram (Mosseri-associated figure, cited secondhand); earlier, Reels were >20% of time in April 2022. — [search summary](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/instagram-reels-statistics/); [iTechPost 2022](https://www.itechpost.com/articles/110362/20220428/reels-now-account-for-more-than-20-percent-of-the-time-users-spend-on-instagram.htm)
- Instagram reported 3 billion monthly users (Sept 2025, 15th anniversary). (aggregator) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)
- HISTORICAL — IGTV: launched 2018 as a YouTube rival; Meta announced shutdown of the standalone app 1 Mar 2022, removed mid-March, videos merged into the main app. Sensor Tower: only ~7M downloads of the app. Reason: low adoption and focus on Reels. — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/guides/igtv/); [HubSpot](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/is-igtv-gone)
- Shop tab removed from the nav in Feb 2023; product tagging in Live ended 16 Mar 2023; Meta refocused on ads (Advantage+ shopping). Live shopping was a tiny share of US e-commerce; Meta's "year of efficiency" cost-cutting. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/02/14/instagram-is-killing-live-shopping-in-march-will-focus-on-ads-instead); [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/instagram-meta-remove-shopping-tab/640040)
- Hashtag following removed Dec 2024. — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/)
- Blend had "mixed reception"; Swap removed. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-news/)

### Inferences
- Pattern: features that live inside existing loops (Reels in the feed, sends in DMs, reposts on profiles) stick; separate tabs/apps for niche behaviour (IGTV, Shop tab, Guides) die. For TNL: put insights, scheduling and reposts inside existing screens, not new top-level tabs.
- Insights matter because creators optimise to whatever is visible: after Views/skip rate appeared, creator advice converged on hooks and sends. TNL should choose metrics that reward what it values (saves to boards, collabs, sends) rather than raw views.

- The 2022 full-screen feed test and its rollback (Mosseri, July 2022, after Kardashian/Jenner "Make Instagram Instagram Again" backlash) is from training knowledge — not re-sourced here; HISTORICAL.
- No Meta-published retention lift numbers for individual features (Map, Reposts, Friends tab, Your Algorithm) were found.
