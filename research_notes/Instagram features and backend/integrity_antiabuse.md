# Integrity & Anti-Abuse: how Instagram/Meta and others stop fake engagement, and what TNL Labs should build

Scope: back-end integrity systems (fake accounts, fake engagement, collusion rings, vote manipulation, reputation gaming, marketplace fraud) and concrete, dependency-free rules for TNL Labs (Node + `node:sqlite`). Current as of 2026-10-07. Repo was read read-only; nothing in `src/` was edited. Repo claims cite file paths and were found by reading code, not by running exploits.

## 1. Meta/Instagram integrity systems (fake accounts, fake engagement, action limits, user-side safety tools)

### Takeaway
Meta layers (a) graph/behavior ML that scores every account (DEC), (b) group-level "lockstep" detectors for coordinated likes/actions (CopyCatch, SynchroTrap), (c) removal of the fake engagement itself rather than just bans, and (d) friction for the attacked user (Limits, Hidden Words, comment warnings, message-request filtering, Teen defaults). The transferable lesson for a small app: remove the *effect* (undo the likes/rep), rate-limit by account age/history, and give targets self-serve filters.

### Cited Findings
- **2018 "like app" crackdown (historical, Nov 2018):** from Nov 19, 2018 Instagram began removing inauthentic likes, follows and comments generated by third-party growth apps; it built ML tools to identify accounts using these services; affected accounts got an in-app message that the activity had been removed and were asked to change their password (because these apps require handing over credentials, and the accounts were then used to generate inauthentic engagement for others). — [Instagram: Reducing Inauthentic Activity](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/reducing-inauthentic-activity-on-instagram/); [WTVR coverage](https://www.wtvr.com/2018/11/19/instagram-cracks-down-on-fake-likes-follows-and-comments)
- **Deep Entity Classification (DEC, USENIX Security 2021; historical but still the public reference):** classifies *all* active Facebook accounts continuously; insight is that "while accounts in isolation may be difficult to classify, their embeddings in the social graph ... are fundamentally difficult for attackers to replicate or manipulate at scale." Extracts >20,000 features, mostly "deep features" = aggregations of neighbours' features (e.g. "average age of an account's friends", p25/p75, % most common category, % empty values). Trains in two stages: large volume of low-precision automated labels, then a small set of high-precision human labels (multi-stage multi-task learning). Credited with an estimated 27% reduction in abusive accounts beyond those caught by other systems. The paper stresses precision over recall because abusive accounts are rare (fake accounts were a small single-digit share of the base). — [Xu et al., DEC paper (USENIX Sec '21)](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~frankli/papers/fb_dec_usenix2021.pdf); press: [MIT Technology Review 2020](https://www.technologyreview.com/s/615313/how-facebook-uses-machine-learning-to-detect-fake-accounts) (reports fake accounts held ~5% of MAU and DEC's four abuse classes: illegitimate, compromised, spammers, scammers — via [Fortune 2020](https://fortune.com/2020/03/04/facebook-a-i-fake-accounts-disinformation))
- **CopyCatch (WWW 2013, historical):** detects lockstep Page-Like fraud using *only* the user–Page bipartite graph and the timestamps of the Like edges: groups of users who like the same set of Pages within the same short time window. Two algorithms (provably convergent iterative + approximate MapReduce). Was in production at Facebook over >1B users. — [CopyCatch, WWW'13 program page](https://archives.iw3c2.org/www2013/program/copycatch-stopping-group-attacks-by-spotting-lockstep-behavior-in-social-networks); [US patent 9077744 "Detection of lockstep behavior"](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9077744)
- **SynchroTrap (CCS 2014, historical):** clusters accounts by similarity of their actions *at around the same time for a sustained period* ("loosely synchronized"); decouples the similarity metric from clustering so it handles both once-only actions (like a Page) and repeatable actions (logins, photo uploads). Reported >99% precision; deployed in five Facebook/Instagram applications; found >2M malicious accounts and 1,156 large campaigns in one month. — [SynchroTrap paper (Cao et al., CCS'14)](https://cse.sc.edu/~huangct/CSCE813F15/SynchroTrap-ccs14.pdf); [Imperial LSDS summary](https://lsds.doc.ic.ac.uk/content/uncovering-large-groups-active-malicious-accounts-online-social-networks)
- **Like farms are measurable:** academic measurement of Facebook like farms (bought likes) characterises them by timing and graph structure. — [Measuring, Characterizing, and Detecting Facebook Like Farms (arXiv 1707.00190)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.00190)
- **Action blocks / "Try again later":** Instagram temporarily blocks a specific action (like, follow, comment, DM) while leaving the rest of the account usable; triggered by exceeding hourly/daily action volume; the threshold reportedly varies with account age, prior violations, automation use and prior activity; usually lifts in 24–48h; Account Status shows active feature limits and when they end. (Secondary sources; Meta does not publish thresholds.) — [Inquirer USA](https://usa.inquirer.net/32124/why-is-instagram-blocking-my-actions); [Combin blog](https://blog.combin.com/action-blocked-on-instagram-what-triggers-and-how-to-get-rid-of-it-70d058a366c9)
- **Limits, Hidden Words, comment warnings (Aug 10, 2021; page updated May 30, 2024):** Limits auto-hides comments and DM requests from "people who don't follow you, or who only recently followed you" — built because pile-ons come from non-followers/recent followers. Hidden Words filters offensive words/phrases/emojis (and low-quality/spammy DM requests) into a Hidden folder. Comment warnings were strengthened to show the strong warning on the *first* attempt; warnings were shown ~1M times/day and ~50% of the time the comment was edited or deleted afterwards. — [Instagram: Introducing New Ways to Protect Our Community from Abuse](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/introducing-new-ways-to-protect-our-community-from-abuse); [Meta newsroom, Aug 2021](https://about.fb.com/news/2021/08/protecting-our-community-from-abuse-on-instagram/)
- **Teen Accounts (Sept 17, 2024; expanded Apr 2025):** private by default for under-16s; messages only from people they already follow/are connected to; tags/mentions from followers only; strictest sensitive-content setting; under-16s need parent approval to loosen; 2025 added no Live and no turning off DM nudity protection without a parent, and expansion to Facebook/Messenger. — [Meta newsroom Apr 2025](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/04/introducing-new-built-in-restrictions-instagram-teen-accounts-expanding-facebook-messenger/); [UK Safer Internet Centre](https://saferinternet.org.uk/blog/teen-accounts-introduced-to-instagram)

### Inferences
- The common pattern: **detect at group level, enforce at edge level.** Meta removes the inauthentic likes/follows (the edges) and notifies, rather than relying only on bans. For TNL this maps cleanly onto the append-only `rep_events` log: write compensating negative events ("voting corrected") instead of deleting.
- DEC's "deep features" idea is cheap at TNL scale as SQL: for each rep receiver, aggregate properties of the *givers* (median giver account age, share of givers with zero own posts, share of givers who signed up within 24h of each other). A receiver whose givers are all brand-new, inactive, single-purpose accounts is suspect even if each giver looks fine alone.
- Limits ("hide interactions from non-followers or recent followers during a spike") is directly implementable as a per-user toggle on comments/DM requests using `follows.created_at`.
- Comment nudges work (~50% edit/delete) and are pure UI + a word list — no ML needed.

### Gaps
- **SybilEdge** (Facebook, 2020; detects fakes from early friend-request behaviour and target acceptance/rejection) and **message requests** internals and **Restrict** were not fetched in this pass; no primary source collected, so no claims made here.
- Meta does not publish action-block thresholds; numbers circulating online are folklore.
- No current (2026) primary source on whether CopyCatch/SynchroTrap are still in production; treat as historical designs.

## 2. Engagement-ring / collusion detection techniques

### Takeaway
At small scale the effective detectors are: (1) per-pair repetition (one giver → one receiver, many actions), (2) reciprocity (A↔B mutual rep flows), (3) temporal lockstep (the same set of givers acting on the same targets within minutes), (4) giver quality (account age, own activity), and (5) shared signup signals (IP, email domain, signup time). All are plain SQL over an event log that records **who gave** — which TNL's `rep_events` currently does not.

### Cited Findings
- Serial voting on Stack Exchange is defined as "when a single user continually votes (up or down) on many of your posts within a short period of time"; such votes are invalid and removed by an automated daily process (plus staff investigations), and appear as a "voting corrected" reputation event. — [Stack Overflow Teams help: serial voting reversed](https://stackoverflowteams.com/help/serial-voting-reversed)
- Stack Exchange deliberately keeps detection details private "because they don't want users optimizing around the various checks." — summarised in search results citing [Stack Overflow blog: More voting anomalies (2009, historical)](https://stackoverflow.blog/2009/03/21/more-voting-anomalies/)
- CopyCatch signal = same set of users × same set of targets × narrow time window (lockstep); SynchroTrap generalises to loosely synchronised repeated actions over sustained periods. — [CopyCatch](https://archives.iw3c2.org/www2013/program/copycatch-stopping-group-attacks-by-spotting-lockstep-behavior-in-social-networks); [SynchroTrap](https://cse.sc.edu/~huangct/CSCE813F15/SynchroTrap-ccs14.pdf)
- DEC: neighbourhood aggregates (giver/friend age distributions etc.) are hard to fake at scale. — [DEC paper](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~frankli/papers/fb_dec_usenix2021.pdf)
- Product Hunt reportedly weights votes by account age/activity, discounts new/single-purpose accounts to ~zero, and flags bursts from accounts created days before a launch and multiple votes from the same IP/device. **Secondary/marketing sources only — Product Hunt has not published this.** — [Demand Curve: How Product Hunt actually works](https://www.demandcurve.com/lessons/how-product-hunt-actually-works); [Flexprice post on producthunt.com](https://www.producthunt.com/p/flexprice/why-your-500-member-community-might-not-help-you-win-on-product-hunt-at-all)
- Disposable email: the community `disposable-email-domains` list (a plain text `disposable_email_blocklist.conf`, updated via PRs and a scheduled job) is the de-facto reference, consumed by ports in Node and other languages; reported at 110k+ domains in one aggregator's count. — [github.com/disposable-email-domains/disposable-email-domains](https://github.com/disposable-email-domains/disposable-email-domains); [Suped overview](https://www.suped.com/knowledge/email-deliverability/technical/where-can-i-find-comprehensive-lists-of-known-disposable-email-domains-to-block)

### Inferences
- A disposable-domain check needs **no new dependency**: vendor the text file into the repo (or a short curated subset) and check `email.split('@')[1]` against a `Set`. Use it as a *weighting signal* (rep from these accounts counts 0 until they have history) rather than a hard signup block, to avoid false positives.
- Device/IP clustering: TNL already reads the unspoofable `CF-Connecting-IP` in `rateLimit()` (`src/server-01-boot.js:45`) but stores no IP anywhere. Storing a **salted hash** of signup IP and last-seen IP per user (not raw IP) gives "N accounts from one IP giving rep to one person" without holding raw personal data.
- At TNL's size (hundreds–low thousands of members) graph ML is overkill; nightly SQL reports + an admin review queue reproduce the useful 80%.

### Gaps
- No primary source on email-domain *reputation* scoring (beyond disposable lists) for small apps.
- Device fingerprinting was not researched; it would need client JS and has privacy cost — not recommended without the owner's approval.

## 3. Voting / contest integrity

### Takeaway
Platforms hide or blur the real tally (Reddit fuzzing, delayed reveal), weight or ignore votes from new/low-history accounts (Product Hunt, reportedly), and reverse bad votes after the fact from an audit log (Stack Exchange). TNL's events already do several of these (server-only counts, freeze, account-age gate, self-vote block, email-verified gate); the gaps are cross-account (sockpuppet) detection and post-hoc vote voiding.

### Cited Findings
- Reddit vote fuzzing: accurate up/down counts can't be shown "because it would make it trivial to figure out a lot of our anti-cheating mechanisms" (admin Deimorz); Reddit replaced up/down counts with "% like it" on June 19, 2014 (historical). Fuzzing adds equal up/down noise so bots can't tell whether their votes were silently discarded. — [Daily Dot, 2014](https://dailydot.com/news/reddit-vote-count-percent-like/)
- Stack Exchange reverses invalid votes daily and shows the reversal openly as "voting corrected". — [serial voting reversed](https://stackoverflowteams.com/help/serial-voting-reversed)
- Product Hunt (reported, secondary): new-account votes count ~zero; same-IP/device votes shadow-discarded; bursts from fresh accounts flagged. — [Demand Curve](https://www.demandcurve.com/lessons/how-product-hunt-actually-works)
- **TNL today (repo):** `evVoteBlock()` requires sign-in, optional email verification (`require_verified`, default 1), optional `min_account_days`, blocks judges voting in the final and blocks voting for your own entry (`src/server-10-events-2-routes.js:28-40`). Votes are one row per (event, stage, matchup, voter, entry) (`src/server-10-events-1-core.js:62-71`). Poll format gives `picks` votes per day keyed by ET day (`server-10-events-2-routes.js` vote route). Vote route rate limit: 120/min per user. CLAUDE.md: counts never leave the server while a stage is open, except poll scoreboard, which freezes for the last `freeze_hours`. — `/home/user/TNL-labs/src/server-10-events-2-routes.js`, `/home/user/TNL-labs/CLAUDE.md`
- **TNL gap (repo):** the `verified` middleware is now a no-op (`function verified(req,res,next){ next(); }`, `src/server-02-auth-feed.js:205`) — so `email_verified` only matters for events because `evVoteBlock` checks `user.email_verified` directly, and only if `require_verified` is on. Registration rate limit is 5/hour per IP (`server-02-auth-feed.js:72`), and the `autoVerify` setting marks everyone verified on arrival — if that is on, the event email gate is also bypassed.

### Inferences
- **Shadow-discard, don't reject:** for suspicious voters (account < N days, disposable domain, same IP-hash as the entrant or as ≥K other voters for the same entry), accept the vote with a 200 and show it in "my votes" but write it with `weight = 0` (new column) so the tally ignores it. Rejecting tells the attacker which signal fired.
- **Audit and void after close, before reveal:** since counts are hidden until close, there is a natural window to run the integrity query, void votes, and publish an admin-visible "votes removed: N" note (Stack Exchange's "voting corrected" transparency).
- **One-person-one-vote without phone verification:** combine account age ≥ 7 days at stage open (not at vote time — prevents "register now, vote in 7 days"), prior non-event activity (≥1 post/like/comment before the event opened), not a disposable email, ≤ 2 voting accounts per IP-hash per entry. Instagram vote links funnel newcomers — expect most fresh signups during an event to be real fans of one entrant; weighting them to zero rather than banning keeps them as members.
- Fuzzing is unnecessary for TNL because counts are already hidden while open; keep it that way.

### Gaps
- Dribbble and dedicated contest platforms' anti-vote-manipulation methods were not researched (no sources fetched).
- No primary Product Hunt documentation found; their weighting is reported only by third parties.

## 4. Reputation-system design lessons (Stack Overflow, Reddit, eBay)

### Takeaway
Hard-to-game reputation systems cap per-day earnings, reverse serial/targeted votes after the fact with a visible ledger entry, and make the cheapest actions (a vote) worth the least relative to expensive ones (accepted answer, paid transaction). TNL already gets two things right — rep only from others, and an append-only log — but rep per action is uncapped and the log doesn't record the giver.

### Cited Findings
- Stack Overflow daily cap: +200 rep/day from votes (and suggested edits); accepted answers, bounties and association bonuses are exempt; when near the cap a partial award is given to reach exactly the cap; introduced Dec 2008 (historical) — without it one top user would have had ~60,000 rep instead of their actual score. — [Stack Overflow blog, Dec 31 2008](https://stackoverflow.blog/2008/12/31/daily-dose-of-daily-reputation-ca/); cap mechanics also in [Stack Overflow Teams: What is reputation](https://stackoverflowteams.com/help/whats-reputation)
- Cap algorithm: on each new award, sum the user's rep awarded in the last 24h and award min(full amount, remaining to cap). — [search summary of SO cap behaviour, Stack Exchange-derived Q&A](https://anspress.net/questions/question/can-i-set-a-maximum-of-200-reputation-per-day/answer/7969/) (secondary)
- Serial votes are reversed by a daily script and logged as "voting corrected". — [serial voting reversed](https://stackoverflowteams.com/help/serial-voting-reversed)
- Reddit hides true vote counts to protect anti-cheat mechanisms. — [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/news/reddit-vote-count-percent-like/)

### Inferences
- The SO cap is exactly the right shape for TNL because rep has money value (fee 10% → 2%). Cap *vote-like* kinds (`like_received`, `share_received`, `pinned`, `sound_used`) per receiver per day, and leave money-backed kinds (`sale_made`, `delivery_confirmed`) and admin `feature` uncapped — mirroring SO's exemption of accepted answers/bounties.
- Add a **per-pair** cap too (SO's serial-vote logic): one giver can move one receiver's rep by at most X per day / Y per 30 days.
- Level 5 (2% fee) is reachable at 560 rep (`src/db.js` LEVELS) = ~94 likes or ~28 accepted collabs or ~187 shares. With no caps that is very cheap for a two-account ring.

### Gaps
- eBay feedback rules (e.g. only one feedback per buyer/seller pair per week counting toward score; feedback only after a real transaction) and Reddit karma throttles were not fetched in this pass — no sourced claims.

## 5. Marketplace fraud on Stripe Connect platforms

### Takeaway
Stripe gives platforms transaction-level Radar plus connected-account risk scoring and actions (review, pause payouts, reserves, reject with reason codes like `fraud_card_casher` — explicitly "the business is both the buyer and the seller"). But Stripe does not know about TNL's rep economy: self-purchase via a second account to farm `sale_made` (+15) and `delivery_confirmed` (+10), and rep surviving refunds, are TNL's to solve.

### Cited Findings
- Radar with Connect: direct charges are screened with the *collecting (connected) account's* Radar config; charges collected on the platform and transferred use the platform's config; transfers between Stripe accounts aren't screened. — [Stripe docs: Radar with Connect](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/radar)
- Platform account-risk tooling: connected-account risk levels `elevated` (score 50–89) and `highest` (≥90, >90% loss probability); custom rules can raise a review / pause payouts; actions include pause payouts, pause payments, reject, set reserves (one-time hold, fixed, rolling), request Stripe Identity verification; reject reasons include `fraud_card_casher` ("the business is both the buyer and the seller and uses stolen credit cards"), `fraud_card_tester` (repeated $0–5 charges), `fraud_no_intent_to_fulfill`. Risk metrics include refund and dispute rates over time. — [Stripe docs: Account fraud prevention (Radar for platforms)](https://docs.stripe.com/radar/radar-for-platforms)
- Radar for Platforms was described as public preview with a per-connected-account fee in search snippets; the current page does not state pricing. — [Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/radar/radar-for-platforms) (status may have changed; verify in Dashboard)
- **TNL today (repo):** Checkout sessions are created with `payment_intent_data.application_fee_amount` and the `Stripe-Account` header, i.e. **direct charges on the seller's connected account** (`src/pay.js:49,144`) — so per Stripe's docs Radar runs with the connected account's config, not the platform's. Self-purchase by the same account is blocked (`l.seller_id === req.user.id` in `server-07-cart.js:33`, `server-07-orders-sharing.js:6`, `server-06-settings-payouts-market.js:357`), but a second account is not. `sale_made` is awarded at settlement (`server-07-orders-sharing.js:118`) and `delivery_confirmed` when the buyer confirms on a paid order (`:193`). There is no Stripe webhook handling and no refund/dispute handling in the code (grep for `refund|dispute|webhook` finds only a notice telling sellers to refund manually), so **refunded or disputed orders keep their rep**.

### Inferences
- Classic self-dealing loop for TNL: seller creates buyer sockpuppet → buys own $1 item (fee paid ≈ 10% of $1 plus Stripe fees) → +15 → confirms delivery → +10 → seller refunds from the Stripe dashboard → rep stays. 23 cycles ≈ level 5. Cost: Stripe processing fees only (refunds generally don't return the fixed fee), a few dollars total, for a permanent fee cut on all future real sales.
- Fixes without new services: (a) minimum price for a sale to earn rep (e.g. ≥ $5), (b) one `sale_made` rep per buyer→seller pair per 30 days, (c) hold `sale_made`/`delivery_confirmed` rep as "pending" for 14 days and only finalise if the order isn't refunded (poll `GET /v1/charges/:id` or `payment_intents/:id` for `amount_refunded`/`disputed` via the existing `stripe()` helper — no new dependency), (d) block rep when buyer and seller share an IP-hash or the buyer account is < 7 days old, (e) compute fee level from rep *excluding* rep from flagged pairs.
- Because charges are direct, Stripe's docs say the connected account's Radar config applies, not the platform's, so the platform's own Radar rules don't screen these charges. Destination charges would put the platform's Radar config in charge. That is a change to how payments work, so it needs `/security-review` and the owner's decision.

### Gaps
- Exact current Radar for Platforms pricing/availability in Oct 2026 not confirmed.
- Refund-abuse patterns specific to digital goods (loops/downloads in `server-08-trust-library.js`) not researched.

## 6. Prioritized, implementable rules for TNL Labs (SQL/JS, no new dependencies)

### Takeaway
Highest-value fixes, in order: (P0) close the uncapped rep faucets found in code (share spam, pin/unpin cycling, collab farming, refund-proof sales), (P0) record the giver on every rep event, (P1) daily per-receiver and per-pair caps with SO-style partial awards, (P1) new-account/low-history giver weighting, (P2) nightly ring/lockstep reports into the existing admin queue with "voting corrected" compensating events, (P2) event vote shadow-weighting. Every rule fits inside `awardRep()` in `src/db.js` plus one nightly job.

### Cited Findings (repo audit — read-only, from code)
- `rep_events(user_id, kind, amount, source_id, created_at)`; comment says `source_id` is "e.g. post id or actor id" but every call site passes a post/order id or `null` — **the giver is never recorded** (`src/db.js:97-104`; call sites in `server-02-auth-feed.js:302,323,348`, `server-09-archive-boards.js:197`, `server-08-trust-library.js:248`, `server-10-collabs-beats-showroom.js:26-27`, `server-07-orders-sharing.js:118,193`). Rep is also denormalised into `users.rep` by `bumpRep`. — `/home/user/TNL-labs/src/db.js`
- **Shares are not deduplicated and have no rate limit:** `POST /api/posts/:id/share` creates a new reshare post and calls `awardRep(original.author_id,"share_received")` every time (`server-02-auth-feed.js:336-348`); deleting the reshare doesn't revoke. Remixes also award `share_received` per remix post (`:299-302`). One sockpuppet resharing one post repeatedly = +3 each. Only `/api/posts` (create) has a 20/min limit; the share route has none. — `/home/user/TNL-labs/src/server-02-auth-feed.js`
- **Pin/unpin cycling:** `pinned` (+3) is awarded when the saver's pin count for the post becomes exactly 1 after insert; unpinning (`DELETE FROM pins`, `server-09-archive-boards.js:222`) does not revoke, so pin → unpin → pin repeats the award (route limit 120/hour). — `/home/user/TNL-labs/src/server-09-archive-boards.js:174-200`
- **Likes are correctly toggled** (unlike calls `revokeRep`), and the likes table enforces one like per (post, user); but there is no route rate limit on `/api/posts/:id/like`. — `server-02-auth-feed.js:313-330`
- **Collabs:** accept awards +20 to *both* sides per post (`server-10-collabs-beats-showroom.js:26-27`). Two accounts can create N posts and cross-invite → +40 per post, uncapped (invite route has no rate limit seen in the read lines).
- `sound_used` (+4) is once per (sample, user) but logs `source_id = null` (`server-08-trust-library.js:244-248`).
- `verified` middleware is a no-op (`server-02-auth-feed.js:205`), so brand-new unconfirmed accounts can give rep immediately.
- Rate limiter is an in-memory sliding window keyed by route+IP or route+user, using `CF-Connecting-IP` (`server-01-boot.js:40-58`); IP is never persisted.
- Admin side: `reports` table exists (`db.js:165`) and an admin "items" queue exists (`server-10-admin.js:168-169`) that can carry new integrity signals.

### Inferences — the rule list (ordered by risk reduction per hour of work)

**P0-1. Record the giver.** Add `actor_id INTEGER` to `rep_events` (migration: `ALTER TABLE rep_events ADD COLUMN actor_id INTEGER`), change `awardRep(userId, kind, sourceId, actorId)`/`revokeRep` signatures, pass `req.user.id` at every call site. Index: `CREATE INDEX idx_rep_actor ON rep_events(actor_id, user_id, created_at)`. Every detector below depends on this. (Old rows stay NULL; that's fine — append-only.)

**P0-2. Close the faucets.**
- Share: award `share_received` only once per (actor, original post) ever — check `SELECT 1 FROM rep_events WHERE user_id=? AND kind='share_received' AND source_id=? AND actor_id=?` before awarding (or a partial unique index). Add `rateLimit({max:20, windowMs:60000, key:"user"})` to the share route. Same once-per-(actor, original) rule for remix credit.
- Pin: award once per (actor, post) ever, regardless of unpinning — same existence check on `rep_events` rather than on current `pins`.
- Like: add a route rate limit (e.g. 120/min) — likes are already one-per-pair.
- Collab: cap `collab_accepted` per unordered pair (e.g. the same two people earn collab rep at most once per 30 days), and require the post to be `is_work = 1` and older than e.g. 1h with non-collaborator engagement — or simply pair-cap.
- Sales: rep only when `amount ≥ RULE.minSaleCents` and only first sale per buyer→seller per 30 days; move `sale_made`/`delivery_confirmed` to a pending state finalised after 14 days if not refunded (re-check via the existing `stripe()` helper).

**P1-1. Daily caps with partial awards (Stack Overflow style)** inside `awardRep`, for "vote-like" kinds only:

```js
const CAPPED = new Set(["like_received","share_received","pinned","sound_used","collab_accepted"]);
const DAY = 864e5, RECEIVER_DAILY_CAP = 60, PAIR_DAILY_CAP = 12, PAIR_30D_CAP = 60;
const sumSince = db.prepare(`SELECT COALESCE(SUM(amount),0) s FROM rep_events
  WHERE user_id=? AND created_at>? AND kind IN ('like_received','share_received','pinned','sound_used','collab_accepted')`);
const pairSince = db.prepare(`SELECT COALESCE(SUM(amount),0) s FROM rep_events
  WHERE user_id=? AND actor_id=? AND created_at>?`);
// in awardRep(userId, kind, sourceId, actorId):
let amount = REP[kind];
if (CAPPED.has(kind)) {
  const now = Date.now();
  const left = Math.min(
    RECEIVER_DAILY_CAP - sumSince.get(userId, now - DAY).s,
    actorId ? PAIR_DAILY_CAP - pairSince.get(userId, actorId, now - DAY).s : Infinity,
    actorId ? PAIR_30D_CAP  - pairSince.get(userId, actorId, now - 30*DAY).s : Infinity);
  amount = Math.max(0, Math.min(amount, left));      // partial award to reach the cap
}
amount = Math.round(amount * giverWeight(actorId)); // P1-2
if (amount <= 0) { insertRepEvent.run(userId, kind, 0, sourceId, actorId, now); return 0; } // keep audit trail
```
Important: `revokeRep` must then revoke **what was actually awarded** for that (user, kind, source, actor), not `REP[kind]` — look it up: `SELECT amount FROM rep_events WHERE user_id=? AND kind=? AND source_id=? AND actor_id=? AND amount>0 ORDER BY id DESC LIMIT 1`. Otherwise like/unlike under a cap becomes a rep *drain* (or, with weights, a faucet). Numbers above are starting points; tune against real data (`SELECT user_id, date(created_at/1000,'unixepoch') d, SUM(amount) FROM rep_events GROUP BY 1,2 ORDER BY 3 DESC`).

**P1-2. Giver weighting (DEC-lite / Product-Hunt-style).** `giverWeight(actorId)`:
- 0 if giver account < 72h old or giver has never posted work and has < 3 distinct people they've interacted with;
- 0.5 if < 14 days old or disposable email domain (vendored list, `Set` lookup);
- 1 otherwise. Admin-flagged givers → 0.
Weight 0 still writes a 0-amount event so the audit trail stays complete. (Rep "only from what others do" is preserved; this only decides how much an *other* is worth.)

**P1-3. Persist hashed IPs.** `users.signup_iphash`, `sessions.iphash` = `sha256(SECRET_SALT + cfIp)` (Node `crypto`, no dependency; salt in Railway Variables). Use only for clustering, never display.

**P2-1. Nightly integrity job → admin queue** (run from the existing server boot timer; write findings to a new `integrity_flags(id, kind, user_id, other_id, score, detail, created_at, handled_at)` table and surface in `server-10-admin.js` items). Queries:

Reciprocal pairs (A gives B and B gives A, heavily):
```sql
SELECT a.actor_id AS u1, a.user_id AS u2,
       SUM(a.amount) AS u1_to_u2,
       (SELECT SUM(b.amount) FROM rep_events b
         WHERE b.actor_id = a.user_id AND b.user_id = a.actor_id
           AND b.created_at > :since AND b.amount > 0) AS u2_to_u1
FROM rep_events a
WHERE a.created_at > :since AND a.amount > 0 AND a.actor_id IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY a.actor_id, a.user_id
HAVING u1_to_u2 >= 30 AND u2_to_u1 >= 30 AND a.actor_id < a.user_id;
```

Concentration — receivers whose rep comes mostly from very few givers (serial-voting analogue):
```sql
WITH g AS (
  SELECT user_id, actor_id, SUM(amount) s FROM rep_events
  WHERE created_at > :since AND amount > 0 AND actor_id IS NOT NULL
  GROUP BY user_id, actor_id)
SELECT user_id, SUM(s) total, MAX(s) top_giver, COUNT(*) givers,
       1.0*MAX(s)/SUM(s) top_share
FROM g GROUP BY user_id
HAVING total >= 60 AND (top_share >= 0.5 OR givers <= 3);
```

Lockstep (CopyCatch-lite): pairs of givers who acted on many of the same posts within 10 minutes of each other:
```sql
SELECT x.actor_id g1, y.actor_id g2, COUNT(DISTINCT x.source_id) shared_posts
FROM rep_events x JOIN rep_events y
  ON y.source_id = x.source_id AND y.kind = x.kind
 AND y.actor_id > x.actor_id
 AND ABS(y.created_at - x.created_at) < 600000
WHERE x.created_at > :since AND x.kind IN ('like_received','share_received','pinned')
GROUP BY g1, g2 HAVING shared_posts >= 5;
```
(Then union connected pairs into groups in JS; a group of ≥3 givers lockstepping on one receiver's posts is a ring.)

Fresh-giver share — receivers whose recent rep is mostly from accounts < 7 days old (DEC "deep feature"):
```sql
SELECT r.user_id, SUM(r.amount) total,
       SUM(CASE WHEN r.created_at - u.created_at < 7*86400000 THEN r.amount END) fresh
FROM rep_events r JOIN users u ON u.id = r.actor_id
WHERE r.created_at > :since AND r.amount > 0
GROUP BY r.user_id HAVING total >= 40 AND 1.0*fresh/total >= 0.6;
```

Shared-IP givers:
```sql
SELECT r.user_id receiver, ua.signup_iphash, COUNT(DISTINCT r.actor_id) givers
FROM rep_events r JOIN users ua ON ua.id = r.actor_id
WHERE r.created_at > :since AND r.amount > 0
GROUP BY receiver, ua.signup_iphash HAVING givers >= 3;
-- plus: giver iphash = receiver iphash (self-dealing via second account)
```

Self-purchase / pair sales:
```sql
SELECT o.seller_id, o.buyer_id, COUNT(*) n, SUM(o.amount_cents) cents,
       MIN(ub.created_at) buyer_joined
FROM orders o JOIN users ub ON ub.id = o.buyer_id
WHERE o.status IN ('paid','shipped','complete') AND o.created_at > :since
GROUP BY o.seller_id, o.buyer_id HAVING n >= 2;
```
(Column names for order amount should be checked against the `orders` schema before use.)
